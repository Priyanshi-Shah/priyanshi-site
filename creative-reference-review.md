## Proposed directions

1. **The Curiosity Conservatory:** a small sculptural world combining a study desk, growing idea tree, display of projects, and hanging speaker badge. A guided scroll visits each zone; labeled navigation goes directly to content. Warm paper, ceramic matcha, brushed metal, glass, and sage vegetation.
2. **The Living Fieldbook:** a physical journal opens and becomes a sequence of miniature pop-up scenes for career, work, community, and travel. Photographs retain prominence; scene transitions are driven by scrolling. A short journal, not a book reader required for every page.
3. **The Museum of Becoming:** an editorial gallery with one interactive sculptural exhibit per chapter. Speech becomes points and connections; projects assemble; speaker passes turn in light; personal photographs unfold. Strongest emphasis on AI and public speaking.

Recommendation: prototype the Living Fieldbook and Curiosity Conservatory as short, actual 3D scenes before choosing. Keep the accepted voice and wording. Use effects to reveal content, maintain clear text navigation, support touch and reduced motion, and put substantive content in searchable pages.
